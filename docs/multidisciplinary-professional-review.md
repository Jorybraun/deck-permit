# Multidisciplinary professional review

Permitly publishes independent, immutable review capabilities for the same `DP-XXXXXXXX` drawing issue. Professional disciplines never inherit one another's authority.

## Structural engineering

The structural review is the permit-readiness gate. Acceptance requires a named EGBC engineer acting in the structural discipline, the engineering practice, Permit to Practice number, current EGBC-directory confirmation, a sealed PDF, and a structured resolution for every generated member, foundation, stair, guard, and connection work package. An Architect AIBC review cannot satisfy this gate.

## Architectural coordination

The optional architectural review lets an Architect AIBC inspect the complete site, existing-condition, plan, elevation, section, stair, guard, and coordination drawing issue. Acceptance records:

- the exact design fingerprint and drawing revision;
- Architect AIBC name and registration number;
- the active AIBC Certificate-of-Practice firm name;
- current individual and firm directory confirmation;
- a sealed architectural review PDF and its SHA-256 digest; and
- sheet-specific redlines or an accepted decision.

The portal cannot select structural members, connections, footings, or resolve structural work packages. The architectural record is included in the controlled permit bundle but does not replace the structural acceptance gate.

## Why architectural review is optional

The Province's [BCAB decision 1977](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/gov/content/industry/construction-industry/building-codes-standards/building-code-appeal-board/building-code-appeal-board-decisions/bcab-1977) states that, apart from Part 9 buildings with common egress systems and firewalls, Division C does not generally require architectural professional design and field review. The authority having jurisdiction or project conditions can still require additional accountability documents.

If an architect is engaged, AIBC's [Architectural Firms guidance](https://aibc.ca/registration/architectural-firms/) requires practice through an active Certificate-of-Practice firm. AIBC's [seal guidance](https://aibc.ca/2025/09/regulatory-review-understanding-the-use-of-the-architects-seal-part-1/) says drawings issued by an architect for permit or third-party reliance must be sealed. The app therefore records both the architect and active firm and treats the separately uploaded sealed PDF as the authority; it never generates or applies a seal.

## Invalidation and package behavior

Any canonical property, site, geometry, opening, elevation, assembly, framing, calculation, or evidence change invalidates both live review links. Imported decisions remain archived against their original fingerprint. The permit archive includes current and stale professional evidence in separate paths, while only a current structural acceptance can satisfy permit readiness.
